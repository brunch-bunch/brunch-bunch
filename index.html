LSQ Brunch Bunch: Places to eat together

Suggested Agenda

for the table, once everyone has ordered

After everyone has ordered and settled in, leaders introduce themselves and welcome everyone. They also warmly ask everyone to introduce themselves.

  1. Friendly welcome to those who are new and those who are regular.
  2. Introduce yourself as a BB leader/volunteer, and say how long you have been at LSQ.
  3. Simple ice breaker:
    1. How long have you been in New York?
    2. What brought you to visit LSQ? / Why do you love LSQ?
  4. Let the group know you will set up a confidential WhatsApp chat to split bills (Venmo), post upcoming church events and share photos. Use the group permission settings shown below.
